Professional Summary

I have 15 years of professional experience spanning AI/ML research, software engineering, education, and IT infrastructure. Currently working as an AI Engineer at a company under NDA, building AI agents with MCP (Model Context Protocol), and as Lead Data Scientist at Cyberette building a multi-modal AI explainability platform (image, audio, and video) — 30+ microservices on Azure App Service. Previously, as Postdoc Researcher at IRB Barcelona (07/2023 – 08/2026), developed Generative AI for drug discovery (dual target inhibitors) using latent diffusion, variational autoencoders, and transformers on HPC clusters.

As an AI/ML consultant I have delivered projects across breast cancer diagnosis from time-series sensor data, pregnancy prediction from microscopic saliva images (CNNs + GCP Vertex AI), and company growth prediction from banking data (3rd place out of 730, BBVA 2022). Earlier research roles at Universitat Rovira i Virgili and Universidad Peruana Cayetano Heredia produced work on SARS-CoV-2 mutation prediction, non-invasive anemia diagnosis, heartbeat detection from infrared video, and stomata classification for drought-resistance studies — resulting in 4 peer-reviewed publications and a WIPO patent.

As an educator I have taught AI/ML, generative AI, prompt engineering, automation (make.com), and Power BI to medical professionals, freelancers, and SME owners at Metodogrupo, Ironhack, and UPCH. My IT background includes 4 years in network administration, Citrix virtualization (XenApp, XenServer, Netscaler), and SQL Server. I hold a Marie Curie PhD fellowship (Nutrigenomics, URV Spain) and a CONCYTEC scholarship (Master's, Peru).
